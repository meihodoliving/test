Directions & access

Guide for visiting Meihodo.

Nagakusa and Ichinokawa area, Aso City, Kumamoto Prefecture. Reach us by national and prefectural roads if driving by car. If you take the train, please contact us about travel from the nearest station.

Pick-up service from the nearest station is also available.

Travel information

Approximate travel times by car, train, and flight.

By car

Fukuoka to Kumamoto IC: about 1 hr 30 min
Kumamoto IC to Meihodo: about 1 hr
Kumamoto City to Meihodo: about 1 hr
*Complimentary parking is available

By train

Kumamoto Station to Ichinokawa Station: about 1 hr 30 min
Oita Station to Aso Station: about 2 hr 30 min
Ichinokawa Station to Meihodo: about 30 min on foot
*If you contact us in advance, we can pick you up at Ichinokawa or Aso Station.

By air

From various cities to Aso Kumamoto Airport
Tokyo (Haneda): about 1 hr 30 min
Osaka (Itami): about 1 hr 10 min

Aso Kumamoto Airport to Meihodo: by car or rental car about 40 min
Airport shuttle to JR Higo-Ōzu Station: about 15 min, then to Ichinokawa Station: about 40 min
