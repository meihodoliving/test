flavors awaken the senses,
experiences carry you beyond the everyday,
a place where the spirit of Japan endures.

At the foot of Mt. Aso, Meihodo offers an immersive retreat where Japanese tradition and nature come together in harmony.

Set across 56,000 square meters of serene grounds, guests can experience traditional Japanese architecture and cultural practices that engage both body and mind. Activities include kyudo (Japanese archery), tameshigiri (sword-cutting practice), karate, sado (tea ceremony), kado (flower arrangement), bonseki, and other traditional arts.

Surrounded by the breathtaking landscapes of Aso, you can leave everyday life behind and find renewed balance in body and mind.

In Aso, discover the spirit of the samurai.

Meihodo offers a range of cultural and martial arts experiences inspired by Japan's warrior traditions. Wear traditional samurai attire and take part in our immersive training program, popular with families, couples, and travelers seeking a deeper connection with Japanese culture.
