Samurai experience

At Meihodo’s Samurai Experience, we invite you on a journey of about two and a half hours, inspired by the training of Edo-period samurai. Practice swordsmanship, tea ceremony, kyūdō, and taiko. Experience the discipline and skill refined through daily practice, and take home a deep connection to Japanese history.

Program flow

How the session unfolds

  • Change into martial arts attire
  • Practice tameshigiri (test cutting)
  • Experience tea ceremony (chanoyu)
  • Learn kyūdō basics
  • Play large taiko drums
  • Conclude with a closing ceremony
