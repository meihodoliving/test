About staying at Meihodo

Meihodo is a cultural estate devoted to passing on traditional Japanese culture, in harmony with nature.

Staying here is different from a stay at a conventional hotel. Its purpose is the time itself — time spent in spaces where Japanese culture lives. For this reason, the buildings, facilities and style of service differ in places from what a typical hotel provides.

Guests who place priority on modern hotel facilities and services may find that Meihodo is not what they expected. We hope instead that you will enjoy what is found only here: spaces with a distinctly Japanese character and the quiet of Aso’s natural surroundings.

We kindly ask that you book with an understanding of what makes Meihodo distinctive.
